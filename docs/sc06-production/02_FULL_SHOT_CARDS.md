# SC06 — Full Shot Cards · 7 уникальных изображений

**Общее правило:** иллюстрация = смысловой драматургический поворот, НЕ одна реплика. Никаких встроенных текстов, облаков или UI. Генерация 16:9, игра сама накладывает корейские реплики. Использовать лица из SC01/SC02 (в первую очередь актуальные игровые файлы в проекте).

## SH01 — Холодная встреча
- **Реплики:** D01–D04
- **Камера:** 35mm wide side angle from café window
- **Image goal:** Medium-wide at a small almost-empty Seoul café on Sunday evening. Yuna at a small round table by window, untouched drink, hands clasped. Sora arrives and takes the opposite chair. Cooler outdoor dusk through windows but natural warm interior lamps. Both recognizable; Yuna is controlled and exhausted; Sora concerned, unaware of revelation. Not a dramatic villain meeting.
- **Эмоциональная цель:** Establish location and emotional distance; no smiles, no crying yet.
- **Непрерывность / negative prompts:** Maintain relative positions: Yuna camera-left / window-side, Sora camera-right / aisle-side; two drinks, two chairs. Avoid flip/flop later.
- **Файл:** `assets/SC06/SC06_SH01.webp`

## SH02 — Подтверждение
- **Реплики:** D05–D10
- **Камера:** 85mm close-up on Sora, Yuna only soft foreground silhouette
- **Image goal:** Sora has sat down. The words Thursday and identity make her freeze; in one clear emotional transition her everyday concern becomes genuine dread. Camera over Yuna shoulder but not a copy of SH01. The confession "yes" must happen on this image.
- **Эмоциональная цель:** First shock lies in Sora losing composure, not in Yuna shouting.
- **Непрерывность / negative prompts:** Keep the same clothing and a coherent eyeline; avoid villain smile or theatrically crying now.
- **Файл:** `assets/SC06/SC06_SH02.webp`

## SH03 — Вопрос об охраннике
- **Реплики:** D11–D15
- **Камера:** 50mm medium, Yuna from slight diagonal, table edge
- **Image goal:** Yuna is unnervingly calm, leaning forward slightly, asking why Sora sent her to the guard. Her gaze is fixed, fingers gripping a cold glass or touching the table. Sora seen as blur/partial in foreground. A more intimate camera than SH01.
- **Эмоциональная цель:** Question motive, sharpen conflict without explosion.
- **Непрерывность / negative prompts:** Yuna not yet shouting, tear-free if possible; darkening expression, subtle tension in jaw.
- **Файл:** `assets/SC06/SC06_SH03.webp`

## SH04 — Признание в намерении
- **Реплики:** D16–D20
- **Камера:** side-on 2-shot wide, camera from opposite wall (strong change from SH03)
- **Image goal:** Both women seated apart across the small table. Sora forced to admit she hoped for breakup but not her own exposure. Sora leans in/looks down, vulnerable yet not excused. Yuna starts to pull back as if stunned by the manipulation. Restrained posture; tension between figures.
- **Эмоциональная цель:** Revelation of Sora trying to influence Yuna, first flash of anger.
- **Непрерывность / negative prompts:** No flashback kiss, no third person, no changed café geometry. No unexplained gestures with props.
- **Файл:** `assets/SC06/SC06_SH04.webp`

## SH05 — Срыв и третья чашка
- **Реплики:** D21–D25
- **Камера:** 35–50mm medium on standing/half-rising Yuna with Sora partly visible sitting
- **Image goal:** Yuna cannot hold herself back; she half-rises from chair, voice raised, hurt anger, eyes wet but not yet fully crying. She remembers their shopping trip and the third mug, no physical mug in café; do not put a third cup into this scene. Sora sits lower, devastated and silent.
- **Эмоциональная цель:** The high anger point grounded in a concrete memory; no melodrama or hysterical posing.
- **Непрерывность / negative prompts:** Exactly two café drinks still untouched. Place chairs and window logically; prefer face and hands over full room redraw.
- **Файл:** `assets/SC06/SC06_SH05.webp`

## SH06 — Ты была моей подругой
- **Реплики:** D26–D30
- **Камера:** 85mm intimate close-up Yuna, severe background blur
- **Image goal:** Yuna sits back down or is seated after the outburst; voice cracks, tears now visible and natural, no grimacing or glamorous crying. Sora distant blurred at edge, also teary but no reach/embrace. Eye-level camera, let hurt dominate over anger.
- **Эмоциональная цель:** Emotional peak: loss of best friend, then exhausted silence.
- **Непрерывность / negative prompts:** Yuna must retain exact facial identity; tears should be restrained, no changing hairstyle mid-shot.
- **Файл:** `assets/SC06/SC06_SH06.webp`

## SH07 — Не звони
- **Реплики:** D31–D34
- **Камера:** wide from doorway looking back at table, or profile at exit
- **Image goal:** Yuna puts on bag and turns away / is exiting the café. Sora remains alone at table with two nearly untouched drinks; posture defeated. The empty chair / separation dominates the frame. It is not the full bleak ending yet: next scene gives Yuna alone at home.
- **Эмоциональная цель:** Cold final boundary and transition to Ending C; visual contrast with Ending B where both women stay.
- **Непрерывность / negative prompts:** Never show Sora chasing Yuna, never reconcile, no ominous text, no sinister grin.
- **Файл:** `assets/SC06/SC06_SH07.webp`

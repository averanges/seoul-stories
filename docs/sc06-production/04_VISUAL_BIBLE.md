# SC06 — Visual Bible · Blocking and Camera Discipline

## Visual anchor priority
1. Faces: Yuna from SC01 / SC02 and Sora from the actual SC02 shopping scene; identical recognizable adult Korean women, independent of hairstyles/clothes.
2. Scene: a **new** small realistically laid out Seoul café near closing. It is not the old apartment, and is not a shopping store.
3. Uniform clothing and hairstyle within SC06. Sunday evening outfit may differ from Saturday SC02. Yuna is visibly drained but begins **dry-eyed**; tearfulness increases only near SH06.
4. Emotional progression trumps beautiful portraits. No smile-cry-smile incoherence; no exaggerated villain gaze from Sora.

Across all shots keep Yuna on the window side (screen left when facing the café) and Sora on the aisle side; small café table between them, two cups/drinks not three. Lock these zones in prompts, use cropped/profiles instead of re-rendering entire room for every shot. Do not mirror establishing shot randomly.

## Cameras and temporal rhythm
- SH01: 35mm establishment window-side 2-shot. Quiet room and controlled interpersonal distance.
- SH02: 85mm Sora reaction; shallow depth of field, no need to draw much room.
- SH03: 50mm diagonal Yuna, table edge; she is checking motive.
- SH04: **deliberately different** side-on two-shot; voices intensify.
- SH05: medium on Yuna rising half-way; Sora lower/sitting, no theatrical shout pose.
- SH06: 85mm emotional Yuna close-up, tears **begin here**, Sora out of focus.
- SH07: wide reverse from café exit; Yuna leaving, Sora alone with two almost untouched drinks.

## Exclusions
- No flashback to the kiss or a hidden woman; no Minsu in this scene.
- No extra coffee cups / false physical "third mug" in café. The third mug is dialogue recalling the shopping episode.
- No hug, handshake, grabbing arms, chasing, triumphant grin, melodramatic collapse, cinematic rain, black-and-white ending, or random camera flip.
- Keep faces consistent at SH05/SH06: the point of the scene depends on recognition.
- Keep lower left/right content reasonably open for the cinematic text overlay when feasible, but do not distort blocking to force empty space.
